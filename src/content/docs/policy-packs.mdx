Policy Packs are collections of domain-specific rules that AgentGate applies
when evaluating an ActionRequest. Each pack defines what kinds of actions and
content are considered risky within its domain, which is what allows the same
core engine (Section 2) to be reused across very different use cases without
changing the guardrail flow itself.\
The three MVP domains — Booking messaging, internal data/code, and productivity
assistant — were chosen as evidence-based risk domains: they are used to
demonstrate realistic risk patterns and are not a claim that AgentGate has
production access to any real company's systems.

| Policy Pack | Risk Coverage |
| :---: | --- |
| Booking messaging policy | External customer send, payment-related content, cancel booking, submit button, customer PII. |
| Code/data protection policy | Source-code exposure, API keys, private keys, credentials, environment files, repository secrets. |
| Productivity assistant policy | Bulk email archive, calendar creation/update, external email send, file access, repository access. |

<DocCard title="Booking messaging policy">

Governs agents operating in customer-facing booking/reservation contexts. It
treats anything that reaches an external customer, touches payment information,
or performs an irreversible action (like cancelling a booking or clicking a
final submit button) as elevated risk, and flags customer PII appearing in
outbound content.

</DocCard>

<DocCard title="Code/data protection policy">

Governs agents with access to source code or infrastructure. It is focused on
preventing secrets and sensitive data from being exposed or exfiltrated —
including API keys, private keys, credentials, .env files, and anything else
that grants access to repositories or systems.

</DocCard>

<DocCard title="Productivity assistant policy">

Governs general-purpose productivity agents (inbox, calendar, files,
repositories). It covers actions that are high-blast-radius because of scale or
reach — bulk operations like archiving many emails at once, calendar changes
that affect other people, messages sent externally, and access to files or
repositories outside the current task's obvious scope.\
Each policy pack maps its risk categories to specific decisions (<DocToken>ALLOW, NEED_APPROVAL, ASK_USER, or block</DocToken>) that the Decision Router enforces, and which route into the Approval Queue and Audit Log as described in Core Concept.

</DocCard>
