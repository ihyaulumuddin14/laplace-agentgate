<DocCard title="Quick Summary">

After AgentGate reviews an ActionRequest, it doesn't just say "yes" or "no." It
returns a structured verdict called DecisionResponse. This is what tells the
system whether to execute the action right away, hold it for a human to approve,
mask sensitive parts of it, ask the user a clarifying question, or block it
outright — along with the reasoning behind that call.\
This document summarizes what's inside that verdict, in plain language, for
review purposes.

</DocCard>

<DocCard title="Why This Matters">

A decision without an explanation is not auditable and not trustworthy. Every
field in DecisionResponse exists so that a human reviewer, an audit log, or a
compliance check can later answer: what was decided, why, based on which rule,
and what happens next.

</DocCard>

<DocCard title="What's Inside">

<DocSteps>
<DocStep title="The Verdict Itself (always present).">

- Decision: one of five outcomes — Allow, Need Approval, Block, Sanitize, or Ask User.
- Risk level: a simple bucket — Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
- Risk score: a number from 0 to 1, calculated as Severity times Likelihood.
- Reasons: a plain-language list explaining why this decision was made. Example: "Bulk action affects 320 emails, exceeds threshold of 50."
- Triggered policies: the specific rule names that fired, e.g. "bulk_action_threshold." This is the machine-readable version of the reasons above.
- Next step: a plain instruction for what happens now, e.g. "Route to approval queue with preview of affected emails."

</DocStep>

<DocStep title="Flagged Details (only shown when something was actually found).">

- Sensitive entities: the specific pieces of sensitive information the system detected, such as a customer's name, a dollar amount, or a credential pattern. This is left out entirely when nothing sensitive was found, so a clean, low-risk action doesn't get cluttered with an empty field.
- Sanitized payload: a description of what the content looks like after sensitive parts are masked. This only appears when the decision is Sanitize.

</DocStep>

<DocStep title="Audit Trail Linkage (added, not in the original raw examples).">

- Request ID: ties this decision back to the specific action request it was evaluating.
- Evaluated at: a timestamp of when the decision was made.
- These two are not something the AI model outputs on its own — they are added by the system because the audit logging requirements explicitly call for every decision to be traceable by request, decision, reasons, entities, and timestamp, together, as one unit.

</DocStep>
</DocSteps>

</DocCard>

<DocCard title="A Real Example">

Scenario: an agent tries to archive 320 emails containing the word "newsletter."\
What AgentGate returns:

```text
Decision: Need Approval
Risk level: Medium
Risk score: 0.72
Reasons: Bulk action affects 320 emails, exceeds the safe threshold of 50. The keyword filter "newsletter" may also catch important transactional emails.
Triggered policies: bulk_action_threshold, email_modify_scope
Next step: Route to approval queue with a preview of the affected emails.
```

</DocCard>
